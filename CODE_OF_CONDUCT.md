# Code of Conduct

StackDoctor welcomes contributors of every background and skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the code and evidence rather than someone's character.
- Give useful, specific feedback and make room for questions from new contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, credentials, or private project material without permission.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized comments, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact methods listed on [MMH-4ALL's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MMH-4ALL). If no private channel is available, open a neutral issue asking for a private contact method; do not publish the complaint or identifying details there.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and keep identifying details private where possible. Responses may include a request to change behavior, removing content, temporarily restricting participation, or permanently excluding a participant. Serious threats or repeated harassment can lead to immediate exclusion.

This policy applies to project spaces and to people representing the project elsewhere. It is a project-specific policy and does not claim external certification or affiliation.
